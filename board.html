Share a ride to Western.

Find a ride to Western’s La Crosse campus or a ride home after class. Request a seat or offer one on your commute.

How it works
  1. Request or offer a ride Choose Request if you need a seat or Offer if you’re driving and have room.
  2. Enter your schedule Pick your community and the days you travel. For To Western, enter when you need to arrive or can get to campus. For From Western, enter when you can leave. Post each direction separately.
  3. Check the ride board Filter by community and direction. If you need a seat, look for offers on your days that fit your time. Select Remove on your own post when it’s out of date.

Example. For an 8 a.m. class, post Tomah → Western with Arrive by 8:00 a.m. Then post Western → Tomah with your after-class departure time.

People signed in to the ride board can see your post type, route, days, and time. The board keeps names and contact details private. Ride preferences are informational only; they do not verify gender or prevent anyone from sending a message. Select Message on someone else’s post to coordinate privately.
