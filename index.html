Pilot details

Who can access the ride board?

Anyone with a confirmed email account can sign in to view posts and coordinate rides.

What information is shared?

Participants can see the route, days, time, and whether someone is requesting or offering a ride. Messages are visible to the two people in the conversation.

What would help a school pilot?

Support with recruiting a small group of riders and drivers, plus an approved email sending domain for notifications.
