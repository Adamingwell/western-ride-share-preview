Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Western Ride Share is an unofficial student-led carpool coordination website. This policy explains the information used by the site.

Information collected

Account registration uses an email address and password through Supabase Authentication. The site stores ride posts (type, community, direction, days, time, and optional ride preference), private messages and their timestamps, conversation read/hidden status, email notification preferences, and signup agreement version and timestamp. Technical service providers may process operational data such as IP addresses and logs.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, show ride listings, deliver private messages, track unread conversations, send optional message notifications, prevent misuse, and operate the service.

Who can see it

Signed-in users can view ride listings and the informational preference on each post. Private messages are accessible to their conversation participants. Email addresses are not shown on public ride cards. Supabase hosts authentication and application data; Resend is used for email delivery. Email notification delivery may involve processing recipient addresses and notification details.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a ride listing does not delete related messages. Removing a conversation hides it from your inbox but does not erase the underlying conversation or the other person's copy. The site does not currently provide a complete self-service account/data deletion process or a fixed data retention period. Do not assume that removing a listing or inbox entry permanently erases data.

Security and choices

Access controls restrict private conversations to participants, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. You can disable email notifications in account settings. Avoid sharing sensitive information in messages.

Changes and questions

This policy may change as the service develops. Before wider launch, the operator should publish a reliable privacy/contact address and a process for access, correction, and deletion requests. This draft should be reviewed for applicable privacy laws and actual provider practices.
