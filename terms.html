Terms of Service

Effective November 14, 2026

Western Ride Share is an unofficial, student-led ride coordination platform. It is not operated, endorsed, or supervised by Western Technical College. Using the site means you agree to these terms.

1. What the service does

The platform lets account holders post ride requests or offers and exchange private messages. It does not provide transportation, employ drivers, inspect vehicles, verify licenses, conduct background checks, arrange insurance, guarantee rides, or supervise trips. Every ride is an independent arrangement between participants.

2. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 years old to use the service. Provide accurate account information, keep your login secure, and do not impersonate another person. Access is currently available to anyone who registers with and confirms an email address; a confirmed email does not verify student status or identity.

3. Ride arrangements and safety

Drivers and passengers are responsible for evaluating one another, confirming trip details, complying with applicable laws, and deciding whether to participate. Drivers must have any legally required license, registration, and insurance. Do not drive impaired, distracted, or unsafely. Consider meeting in a public place, sharing trip details with someone you trust, and declining any ride that feels unsafe. In an emergency, contact emergency services.

4. Ride preferences

Ride posters may select an informational gender-related comfort preference. These preferences do not verify anyone's gender, restrict who can send a message, or guarantee safety. Communicate respectfully and follow applicable law.

5. Conduct and content

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate unlawfully, stalk, impersonate, scam, share another person's private information without permission, post deceptive rides, or use the service for illegal activity. Do not send unsolicited commercial messages. Ride details and messages must be lawful and respectful.

6. Cancellations and communications

Confirm pickup arrangements directly with the other participant. Notify them promptly if your plans change. Removing a ride listing does not remove existing private conversations. Removing a conversation from your inbox does not remove the other participant's copy; a later reply may make it visible again.

7. Service availability and moderation

The service may be modified, interrupted, or discontinued. Access or content may be restricted when necessary to protect users or enforce these terms. No specific response time or uninterrupted availability is promised.

8. Responsibility and limitations

Transportation carries risks, including injury, loss, and misconduct. The platform is provided on an as-available basis, without promises about other users or ride outcomes. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the operator is not responsible for independently arranged rides or participants' actions. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liabilities that cannot legally be excluded.

See the Privacy Policy for information about personal data. These terms may be updated; material changes should be communicated and may require renewed acceptance.
